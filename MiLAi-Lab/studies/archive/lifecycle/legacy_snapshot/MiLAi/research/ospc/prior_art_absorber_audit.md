## Candidate after noun erasure

After removing the name “OSPC”, the candidate is: reserve enough context budget for a typed unresolved-state record; keep its identity, target, positive and negative source pointers, resolution predicate and critical dependencies; reject or fall back when those fields do not survive compression.

That description is directly implementable as a conventional typed-state/structured-eviction policy with mandatory fields. The candidate has no separation witness from that policy in the frozen pilot.

## Strongest absorber

The strongest absorber is not an external framework. It is the local `typed_state` / `static_open_issue` policy that marks the exact unresolved-state fields mandatory and reports infeasible below their minimum cost. Under the same tokenizer, input, frozen labels and total budget, it equals the full candidate on:

- OpenIssue identity recall;
- support/contradiction branch and Evidence-pointer recall;
- false-closure rate;
- legal-resolution accuracy;
- later-Episode task success;
- infeasible rate and retained-token distribution.

The full candidate performs additional validator checks without pilot gain. `ospc_no_validator` is also equal on all frozen primary fields, so this pilot supplies no validator separation witness.

## Hard-falsifier decision

Decision: **ABANDON the OSPC novelty claim**.

Triggered conditions:

1. `HF-01`: the strongest typed-state baseline is equal at the same total budget;
2. `HF-02`: simply keeping the complete OpenIssue field is equal;
3. `HF-06`: validator work has no measured task or safety gain in the pilot.

This decision does not discard the engineering invariant. MiLAi should continue protecting live OpenIssue fields in Minimal Context, under the existing product contract, without presenting that practice as a new OSPC algorithm. Reopening research would require non-synthetic traces, an independently frozen downstream task, a separation witness against typed state, and a broader literature review. It would still need a new decision before any product integration.
