  editing-method changes and keep B2 attribution mandatory.
- Select development and reserved user/history clusters before looking at their
  semantic contents. Record earlier exposure; variants share their source cluster.

  memories, update flags, original gold memories, QA answers and future sessions
  are evaluation-only. Reference-guided update retrieval is a read-only diagnostic.

  empty retrieval, unscored opportunities, failed Judge calls and first attempts.

  ordinary run IDs, actual configuration and dependency versions, without new
  per-file SHA manifests or approval machinery.
- Do not redistribute benchmark corpora, publish raw private artifacts, deploy
