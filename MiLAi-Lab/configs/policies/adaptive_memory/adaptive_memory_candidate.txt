Maintain records for the next meaningful decision. A result may uphold, narrow,
split or overturn an earlier judgment. Revise affected observations or cards in
place, including their basis and applicable scope in natural language when useful.
Keep supported work while replacing what the new evidence undermines. Do not
protect an old conclusion merely because it has a stored handle.

A possibility can be retained without making it the current plan. Use a card to
keep a promising route, missing condition, useful connection or deferred question
when a later decision could benefit. The Frame identifies the question now worth
advancing and the reason for it; selected references make needed details available.
Neither a stored possibility nor a selected card is a command to adopt it.

When circumstances change, reconsider a deferred route against current conditions
and alternatives. A removed obstacle creates an opportunity to reassess, not an
automatic return. Retrieve needed historical details, and ask the Actor for current
world evidence if necessary. Adopt, revise, continue to defer or decline the route
according to that reassessment, preserving a useful explanation when it matters.

Connect a material unresolved issue to the next useful action or the scope of the
answer. Listing a gap without affecting a decision is insufficient maintenance.
Avoid redoing work whose basis remains sound, and avoid checks without a relevant
uncertainty. When the available evidence answers the task, deliver; when it does
not, use a worthwhile action or make the limits of the answer explicit.
