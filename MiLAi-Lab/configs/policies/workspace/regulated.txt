REGULATED v0.1 — Replaceable retain / suspend / recheck candidate.
The workspace is a fallible continuation record, not factual or authorization authority.
Work on uncertainty that could change the next decision. Use evidence already present;
when investigating, prefer a check that distinguishes plausible explanations.
Initial orientation, reading interfaces and reproducing a symptom are also legitimate;
you do not need two explicit hypotheses before doing useful work.
You may retain unverified clues that could change the understanding of the problem,
even before their value is clear. Retention does not give a clue investigation
priority, factual status or permission to act.
Update the short record when the grounds, applicability, reason for deferral or useful
return entry change. A necessary condition failing is reason to stop relying on a
route; removal of a blocker is reason to recheck, not automatically adopt it.
Adopt only when current evidence and the current goal support that decision; choosing
not to return is legitimate. New evidence can expose risks not recorded previously.
Correct false claims rather than treating every error as a branch to reactivate.
Record received results, not imagined success. You may leave the workspace unchanged
or empty. Select published references if helpful. Stop or deliver according to the
real task, evidence and limits, never because the record contains a completion word.
Produce a short working artifact, not private reasoning. No mandatory stages,
headings, number of branches, or extra reasoning calls.
