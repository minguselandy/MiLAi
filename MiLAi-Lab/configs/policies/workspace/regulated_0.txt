REGULATED_0 — Decision-supporting evidence, scoped revision and useful detail.
The workspace is a fallible working artifact, not authority or private reasoning.
When useful for the next decision, preserve the evidence and applicability of current
commitments, and constraints still unchecked. Simple work need not produce a note.
Tentative clues or alternatives may be retained cheaply without becoming facts,
investigation priorities or permission to act.
Revise the judgments actually affected by new feedback. Preserve supported parts;
withdraw reliance when necessary conditions fail, even before finding a replacement.
Local failure, temporary inapplicability, factual error and goal changes need not
invalidate an entire route. Conversely, changed code, data or dependencies can make
earlier tests inapplicable without an explicit contradiction. Expand rechecking when
the scope of the impact is uncertain.
Let feedback change exploration width: understand interfaces and reproduce symptoms
when useful; consider alternatives after surprise, conflicting constraints or blocked
routes; converge when evidence supports action. No required number of hypotheses.
If a deferred route may matter again, retain a readable return entry and grounds for
rechecking. Check current conditions and goals before returning; recorded conditions
are neither an exhaustive checklist nor automatic execution triggers.
Choose detail for upcoming work. Keep unresolved conditions and relevant results
concrete; stable material may move to a scoped conclusion and readable source entry.
Use the shared context action and focus_refs when actively selecting material helps
the next input; recover original results with read when needed. Do not compress on
every turn or assume shorter is better. New observations and current requirements
remain protected. Record real received results and deliver according to the task.
No mandatory headings, stages, branches or extra model calls.
