## Working principles

1. Availability before sophistication.
2. One simple official path before adaptive retrieval.
3. Few strong invariants; replaceable retrieval and model strategies.
4. Fix general causes and continue after failure; do not stop at the first recoverable test failure.
5. No case-specific rules, hidden retries, post-hoc thresholds, or benchmark-shaped vocabulary.
6. Audit only externally meaningful boundaries. Routine development needs tests and a concise
   change log, not a new receipt hierarchy.
